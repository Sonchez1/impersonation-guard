Know why it's a scam, before you click.

Inbox verdicts with evidence. A stop before you type a password or code into a fake page. Everything runs on your computer.

SCAMThis message is a scam.
  • The name says "LinkedIn Support" but the address is gmail.com, which LinkedIn does not use.
  • A link goes to "linkedin-verify.app", which has "LinkedIn" glued to other words.
  • It asks you to read back a code. Real support teams never ask for these.
